## Method edition

EASI/Hanifin 2001 + official HOME guide (header “EASI guidance December14”): 4 regions and 4 signs; regional averages allow 1.5 and 2.5, but not 0.5; weights \<8 / ≥8 years; total 0–72

## Documented formula

In each region: (erythema + edema/papulation + excoriation + lichenification) × area (0 to 6) × weight. The regional average intensity of each sign allows 0, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5 or 3; 0.5 is not allowed. Weights from age 8: head 0.1; upper limbs 0.2; trunk 0.3; lower limbs 0.4. Below age 8: 0.2; 0.2; 0.3; 0.3. EASI ranges from 0 to 72.

## Limits and population

The 2001 EASI study assessed reliability among trained dermatologists in people with atopic dermatitis, in groups aged \<8 and ≥8 years. The version used must apply the corresponding age weights and adequately assess signs. Instrument reliability is not diagnostic validation or language approval for local translations. The official HOME guide directs assessment of the average intensity of each sign across the entire region: it allows 1.5 and 2.5, but not 0.5, because any present sign must score at least 1. Assessment includes only inflammatory eczema; xerosis, ichthyosis, keratosis pilaris, urticaria, infection without underlying eczema and post-inflammatory pigment changes do not enter the score. The technical check here covers the weighted sum, including the permitted half points, and does not approve severity cutoffs, clinical decisions, official translations or instrument rights.

Run node test.cjs in the root directory of this repository to repeat the recorded synthetic cases. Original inputs, expectations and tolerances are preserved. Technical tests do not constitute clinical validation.

```sh
node test.cjs
```
